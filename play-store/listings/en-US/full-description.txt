Everything you need to remember, organized in one place.

Secretaria turns everyday ideas, tasks and plans into clear lists and useful reminders. Make a grocery list, prepare a trip, organize a project or write something down before it slips your mind.

LISTS AND NOTES
• Create lists and add notes with a title, details and color.
• Keep items in order when sequence matters.
• Group related lists and archive the ones you no longer need.
• Search and sort to find what you need quickly.

REMINDERS
• Create reminders with an optional date and time.
• Get a notification when the time comes.
• Mark tasks as complete and review their history.
• Reorder pending items around your priorities.

SHARE AND COLLABORATE
• Add friends with a personal code.
• Share full lists or individual reminders.
• Get notified when someone shares something with you.
• See clearly who has access.

YOUR ROUTINE, YOUR WAY
• Sign in with email or Google, or continue as a guest.
• Use the interface in English or Spanish.
• Enjoy a modern experience built with Kotlin Multiplatform.
• Ads only appear in the optional “Support the creator” section.

Secretaria helps you clear your mind without losing control of your plans.
